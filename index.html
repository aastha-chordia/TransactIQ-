How TransactIQ decides

A Random Forest model estimates transaction risk from six synthetic features. Deterministic policy rules can pause high-value, mismatched or restricted-destination payments. SHAP contributions are returned when available; otherwise feature importance is shown.

Educational prototype only. No real funds are transferred. Country restrictions, currency conversion and provider performance are illustrative demo rules, not live regulatory or market data.
